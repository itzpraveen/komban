Komban

A Push / Pull / Legs gym coach for iPhone and Apple Watch, in English and Malayalam

SupportPrivacy

Komban plans your training week, tells you the weight and reps to beat each time, shows every exercise as an animation with what to watch for, and logs your sets on the phone or the wrist. It also keeps a simple food, protein and body-weight log, and counts what you've lifted in Kombans (tuskers): one Komban is 4,000 kg.

Need help? Email hello@zphiloso.com. Please say which iPhone and Apple Watch you have. If the app offers to share its error log (Me tab), attaching it helps.

Questions

Where are my workouts kept?

Only on your iPhone, and in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if that's on. Komban has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

In the Me tab, open Backup and save a backup file somewhere safe (Files, Drive, or a message to yourself). On the new phone, choose Restore file and pick it. A new install also offers to restore on its first screen.

How do I get the Apple Watch app?

It comes with the iPhone app. If it isn't on your watch, open the Watch app on the iPhone, find Komban under Available Apps and tap Install. Open Komban on the iPhone once so your plan reaches the watch.

How do I switch between English and Malayalam?

Me tab → Language. The watch follows the phone.

Heart rate isn't showing on the watch

The watch needs permission to use Apple Health. On the iPhone go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Komban and turn the permissions on. Komban still logs your sets without it.

The weights don't match my gym

In Me, set how far apart your gym's dumbbells are and the smallest plates it has. The − / + buttons and the weights Komban suggests follow them.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on your iPhone. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Safety

Komban is a training log and coach, not medical advice. Stop a set if anything hurts sharply. If you have a heart or blood-pressure condition, are pregnant, or are coming back from an injury or surgery, check with a doctor before you start.

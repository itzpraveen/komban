Privacy Policy

Komban, the gym coach for iPhone and Apple Watch

SupportPrivacy

Effective 27 November 2026.

In short: Komban doesn't collect any data about you. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you log stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and leaves them only when you choose to share it.

Who we are

Komban is made by ZPHILOSO. Questions about this policy: hello@zphiloso.com.

What the app keeps, and where

To coach you, Komban saves what you enter in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps and rest times;
  • your body log: weigh-ins and any body measurements you add;
  • your daily log: food, protein and calories, water and habits;
  • your settings: goal, training days, body weight, height (if you give it), language, theme and the equipment at your gym;
  • notes you write and foods you add.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Komban has no servers and doesn't connect to the internet. If you've turned on iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple includes the app's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. Apple runs those backups, not us.

Apple Watch

The watch app gets your plan from your iPhone and sends the sets you log back to it, directly between your own devices through Apple's Watch Connectivity. Nothing goes through a server.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the watch app saves your gym sessions to Apple Health as strength workouts. During a workout it reads your heart rate and active energy to show them on the watch. Komban doesn't read any other Health data. Health data isn't used for advertising or marketing, isn't shared with anyone and isn't sold. Komban works without Health access. You can change this any time in the iPhone's Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Komban.

Notifications

Rest timers and reminders are scheduled on your devices. No notification goes through a server.

When you share something

Some things leave the app only when you tap to send them, through the iPhone's share sheet, to wherever you pick:

  • Backup: a file with your workouts, logs and settings, for you to keep or restore later.
  • Workout summary: a text of the workout you just finished.
  • Error log: if the app ran into a problem, it offers to share its error log. The log holds technical details only, no workouts or food. It reaches us only if you choose to send it to us.

Deleting your data

Deleting Komban from your iPhone deletes everything it saved there. Workouts the watch saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them in the Health app.

Children

Komban collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Komban ever starts collecting data, the policy will say so first, and the app will ask you before it does.
